## Capture format

- phone vertical 9:16
- rear camera
- no beauty filters, AI enhancement, portrait blur, or heavy exposure that hides condition
- if available: 4K/30 fps; otherwise 1080p/30 fps
- hold each usable shot about 2–4 seconds before moving
- keep movements slow enough for later cropping/subtitles
- record natural engine/door/interior sound where useful
- avoid visible customer faces/documents/phone numbers; do not expose VIN publicly unless specifically approved

## Shoot the same core set for each vehicle

1. Front three-quarter hero — full car, 2–4 s.
2. Rear three-quarter — full car, 2–4 s.
3. Side profile — stable or slow walk, 2–4 s.
4. Front/interior transition — open driver door and reveal cabin.
5. Interior wide — dashboard + front seats.
6. Odometer/dashboard — mileage readable and warning lights not hidden.
7. Engine start / idle — capture sound and dashboard state.
8. Wheels/tyres — one front and one rear wheel.
9. Trunk / cargo area.
10. Condition proof — deliberately film visible dents, scratches, paint mismatch, warning lights or other material defects if present; do not avoid them.
11. Clean walkaround — one continuous 8–15 s clip for editing flexibility.
12. Optional safe movement shot — vehicle arriving/leaving slowly in a safe private/showroom area; otherwise skip.
