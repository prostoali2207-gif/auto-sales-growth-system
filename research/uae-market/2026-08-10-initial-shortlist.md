# UAE Automotive Social Media Research — Initial Shortlist

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: Phase 1 — candidate discovery

## Objective
Build a fact-based benchmark set of UAE automotive sellers whose Instagram / YouTube / short-form content can be analyzed for repeatable sales and attention mechanisms.

## Initial benchmark candidates

### Core used-car / dealership benchmarks
1. Alba Cars — broad used-car dealer; strong trust/finance/warranty positioning; active on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
2. The Elite Cars — premium/luxury multi-brand used-car dealership.
3. Expat Motors — high-review-volume used-car retailer; useful trust/conversion benchmark.
4. Linda Cars — used-car retailer; useful mid-market benchmark.
5. Pinoy Used Cars — lower/mid-price used-car benchmark and first-time-buyer positioning.
6. KK Cars Trading — luxury pre-owned, sourcing, consignment, trade-in and finance.
7. One Stop Used Cars — luxury used-car collection and social-media-forward inventory presentation.

### High-reach luxury / attention benchmarks
10. F1rst Motors — hypercar/luxury dealership with very large social audience; useful for reach mechanics, not necessarily directly transferable to normal stock.
11. VIP Motors — major luxury/supercar dealership on Sheikh Zayed Road.

### Market/platform benchmarks

13. DubiCars — automotive marketplace/content benchmark.
14. CARS24 UAE — digital used-car buying/selling benchmark.
15. AutoTrader UAE — content + marketplace benchmark.

## Current evidence worth keeping

- F1rst Motors is a clear reach outlier: public third-party channel statistics in July 2026 show about 1.85M YouTube subscribers and about 175M total views. One older Koenigsegg ASMR short/video is reported at more than 28M views and >100x the channel norm. This makes F1rst useful for studying spectacle, sensory hooks, rarity and close-up vehicle content, but dangerous as a direct template for ordinary inventory.
- F1rst Motors' own site says it was established in 2021 and has sold more than 1,000 vehicles. DubiCars reported its social footprint at roughly 2.9M Instagram followers, 1.6M TikTok followers and over 1M YouTube subscribers when their partnership was announced. Treat those follower numbers as time-specific, not current truth.
- Alba Cars is a stronger conversion/trust benchmark than F1rst for ordinary used-car sales. Its current site emphasizes 150+ point inspection, mileage verification, RTA certification, one-year warranty, finance, sell-your-car flow and WhatsApp contact.
- Dubai automotive sales content commonly routes attention to WhatsApp/DM rather than trying to complete the transaction inside social media. This is consistent with current UAE automotive marketing practice and should be tested in our own funnel.

## Research rules for Phase 2

For each account collect the latest 30–50 short-form posts when technically possible:
- publish date
- views / plays
- likes
- comments
- duration
- caption
- opening 1–3 seconds / hook
- spoken hook or transcript
- vehicle / price / mileage / payment mention
- format category
- CTA
- buyer questions in comments
- relative performance versus that account's own median

Do not rank content by raw views alone. A 100k-view post on a small account can be more informative than a 500k-view post on a multi-million-follower account.

## Hypotheses to test, not assume

1. Price/payment in the first 1–3 seconds improves lead-oriented performance.
2. Presenter-led walkarounds outperform pure cinematic inventory clips for trust and enquiries.
3. Rare-car sensory/ASMR content is strong for reach but weakly transferable to ordinary used-car inventory.
4. Inspection/warranty/history proof is a recurring trust mechanic in UAE used-car sales.
5. WhatsApp is the dominant conversion handoff after Instagram/TikTok/YouTube discovery.

None of these hypotheses should become a playbook until repeated evidence is found across multiple accounts/posts.
