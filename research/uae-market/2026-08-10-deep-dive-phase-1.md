# UAE Automotive Social Market — Deep Dive Phase 1

Date: 2026-08-10
Status: evidence collection in progress

## Objective
Move from qualitative competitor observations to evidence-backed content and sales patterns.

## Current evidence

### F1RST MOTORS — reach benchmark
- YouTube: ~1.85M subscribers, ~174.94M total views, ~1.29K videos (vidIQ, updated 2026-07-25).
- HypeAuditor July 2026 snapshot: average Shorts views ~16.6K; average video views ~36.2K; engagement rate 6.09%.
- Top observed Short: ASMR Koenigsegg Jesko Attack “Odin” — 28.65M views, >100x channel outlier.
- Interpretation: spectacular inventory + sensory/ASMR presentation is a proven reach mechanic for this luxury/hypercar account. It is NOT sufficient evidence that the same mechanic sells ordinary inventory.

### The Elite Cars — trust/conversion architecture
Official site emphasizes:
- hand-selected luxury inventory;
- finance;
- trade-in;
- immediate ownership;
- customer testimonials;
- showroom/contact paths.
Interpretation: content should not be evaluated only by reach. The downstream buying proposition matters.

### Alba Cars — trust/conversion architecture
Official contact/site material emphasizes:
- used-car condition;
- inspection and verification;
- direct call and WhatsApp contact;
- physical showroom.
Recent DubaiPetrolHeads discussion (2026-07-23) also shows buyers explicitly asking peers about dealer trust before visiting.
Interpretation: buyer-risk reduction is a content opportunity: condition, inspection, history, negotiation clarity, admin process.

### CarSwitch / DubiCars — marketplace benchmarks
- CarSwitch Instagram public third-party snapshot: ~26.1K followers and 2.3K media posts (SocialBlade snapshot).
- DubiCars third-party snapshot: ~47.1K followers, 4.1K posts; reported profile engagement below peer benchmark in that snapshot.
Interpretation: high posting volume / follower count alone cannot be treated as proof of an effective sales content system.

## Buyer-intent signals from UAE community discussions
Recurring concerns found in Dubai/UAE car-buying discussions:
1. Actual mechanical condition vs listing claims.
2. Mileage authenticity / rollback risk.
3. Service history.
4. Dealer markup vs owner price.
5. Quality and limitations of inspections.
6. Ability to test drive / independently inspect.
7. Dealer reputation and post-sale accountability.

These are candidate TRUST and LEAD content themes because they address concrete purchase friction rather than generic entertainment.

## Working taxonomy
Every observed post/video will be classified as one primary job:
- REACH — earns attention from cold audience.
- TRUST — reduces perceived purchase risk.
- LEAD — creates an enquiry/contact action.
- DIRECT SALE — sells a specific vehicle with concrete commercial information.

## Evidence rule
Do not promote a format into a playbook until either:
- it repeats across >=3 independent dealer accounts; or
- >=5 comparable posts within one account materially outperform that account's baseline.
One viral outlier is never enough.

## Data gap discovered
Open web search does not reliably expose 30–50 current Instagram Reel-level records per dealer. Therefore the next technical requirement is structured collection (e.g. Apify Instagram Reel Scraper or equivalent) before claiming Reel-level quantitative patterns.
