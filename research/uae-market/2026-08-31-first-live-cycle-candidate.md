## Candidate assessment

### AM-016 — TEST_CANDIDATE
Why this vehicle is useful for the first experiment:
1. Exact vehicle identity and current availability are verified.
2. The material trust/risk fact (front-end salvage history) is known and can be disclosed consistently in both variants.
3. A current UAE marketplace cohort exists, so the vehicle is not being tested in a context with zero observable market presence.
4. The experiment can isolate one meaningful variable: when the verified history is disclosed.
5. The experiment can avoid unsupported market-price claims entirely.

This is NOT a finding that Sonata has the strongest demand or best economics. It is a finding that AM-016 currently offers the cleanest evidence-backed test surface for a transparency mechanism.
