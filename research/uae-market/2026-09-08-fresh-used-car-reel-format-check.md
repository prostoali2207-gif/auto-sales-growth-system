## Observed facts

### O1 — Short visual inventory format is currently used on ordinary stock

`@alaramcars` recent inventory Reels include ordinary vehicles such as Nissan Sunny, Kia Picanto, Renault Duster and other non-hypercar stock in roughly 15–28 second clips.

Direct verification:
- 2021 Nissan Sunny Reel, published 2026-08-29: 14.905 s.
- Audio transcription detected zero speech segments.
- Caption carries basic vehicle/spec information.

Support scope: one directly audio-verified Sunny plus repeated nearby account inventory posts with similar short runtimes.

### O2 — Very short silent micro-clips are also currently used

`@alqimma_usedcars_17` published multiple 5-second Reel assets around inventory offers.

Direct verification:
- BAIC BJ40 Reel, published 2026-09-03: 5.034 s.
- Media had no audio stream.
- Detailed price/mileage/spec/finance information was carried in the caption.

This establishes the existence of the format, not its effectiveness.

### O3 — Long spoken walkaround format is currently used

`@platform.motors` recent Reels commonly run approximately 60–100 seconds.

Direct verification:
- Reel published 2026-08-25: 72.003 s.
- Arabic speech is continuous through most of the video.
- The presenter/walkaround discusses vehicle identity/year, paint/condition-related information, equipment/interior, mileage and financing-related information.

This is a real information-dense spoken vehicle presentation, not a silent cinematic montage.

### O4 — The current market therefore contains at least two materially different inventory execution families

Observed families:
1. compact visual/silent inventory presentation;
2. longer spoken walkaround/explanation.

The evidence does not justify calling either one the universal best format.

## Prior project evidence that constrains interpretation

The 2026-08-11 200-post competitor dataset showed:
- hypercar rarity/spectacle creates extreme outliers and is not transferable evidence for ordinary inventory;
- generic cinematic/detail presentation appeared closer to baseline in sampled luxury accounts;
- commercially transferable above-baseline examples included process proof, buyer education, explicit customer jobs and a sharp truthful reason to care about the exact vehicle;
- a conventional presenter standing beside cars appeared useful for trust/education but was weaker evidence as a pure reach hook than an event/problem/reveal;
- the prior frame analysis explicitly warned against a slow generic beauty pan as the default opening.

These older results are supporting context, not fresh causal evidence for AM Motors.

## Epistemic classification

### OBSERVED_FACT
Both silent/visual and spoken-walkaround formats are currently being used by UAE used-car sellers.

### OBSERVED_FACT
The directly inspected examples differ substantially in duration and information delivery.

### INFERENCE
The two formats plausibly serve different communication jobs: visual inventory can communicate availability/product quickly; spoken walkaround can carry more explanation/proof.

This is an inference because public competitor media does not expose their qualified-lead or appointment outcomes.

### HYPOTHESIS
For ordinary AM Motors stock, the choice of format may materially change qualified-inquiry efficiency and lead quality. The business should test the format bundles rather than standardize one from competitor imitation.

## Counterevidence / limits

- Newly tracked competitors do not provide a valid fresh historical performance baseline in Sociality yet.
- Public views/likes would still be proxy outcomes, not qualified leads, appointments or sales.
- The format families are bundles: spoken walkaround usually also changes duration, information density and audio. A bundle-level test can choose a production format but cannot isolate the causal effect of speech alone.
- Three fresh accounts are not a representative census of UAE dealers.
- Account brand, inventory, language and distribution can confound cross-account comparisons.

## Bounded implication for Strategist

`TEST_CANDIDATE`: compare two clearly defined inventory-format bundles on the same AM Motors vehicle/offer while preserving verified commercial facts, CTA path, distribution conditions and tracking as tightly as practical.

Do not copy competitor wording, claims or visual expression. The decision target is AM Motors qualified demand, not competitor views.

## Research stopping

STOP_WITH_LIMITATION.

The immediate uncertainty is no longer “do UAE sellers use spoken or silent formats?” — both are observed.

The remaining business-relevant uncertainty is “which format produces better qualified demand for AM Motors?” That requires an internal experiment, not more competitor browsing.
