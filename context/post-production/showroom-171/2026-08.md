# Showroom 171 Post-Production Context — 2026-08

Status: project operating context. Snapshot: 2026-08-17.

## Established

- Used-car retail at Ajman Auto Market, Showroom 171.
- Instagram is the primary current surface; YouTube and Telegram are connected funnel surfaces.
- Source footage is commonly vertical phone video captured by a small team.
- Human creative, commercial-fact and publication approval remain required.
- Commercial claims must come from verified business facts, not old reels or chat memory.
- A silent deliverable is valid when explicitly approved and all essential meaning is visually available.

## Not established

- A single mandatory NLE, paid AI editor or subscription.
- Stable brand font, motion system, music library or LUT.
- Permanent platform export values or safe zones.
- Permission to upload customer, vehicle-document or private source media to third-party AI services.

## Tool decision

Choose the lowest-total-cost eligible toolchain for the actual asset and deadline. Eligibility comes before price: source privacy/upload rights, required quality, render ability, artifact inspection, reproducibility and device availability must pass first.

Do not purchase or subscribe by default. A free/mobile tool is not acceptable if it cannot preserve truth or produce verifiable output; an expensive tool is not justified when a reproducible local edit is sufficient.

## Pre-publication truth recheck

Reconfirm current vehicle identity, availability, approved price, mileage and every material condition/spec/finance/warranty claim after rendering and immediately before publication. Re-edit when the approved fact changes; do not merely explain a stale on-screen claim in the caption.

## Revalidation triggers

Platform placement/export changes; a new editor/toolchain; third-party AI upload; brand system approval; new privacy/rights practice; changed business approval flow; recurrent render or caption failure; new language workflow.
