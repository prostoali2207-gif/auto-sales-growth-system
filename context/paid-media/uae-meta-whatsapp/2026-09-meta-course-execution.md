## Mandatory Meta execution gate

Before recommending or executing a Meta campaign change, the Paid Media specialist must explicitly check:

1. **Business objective → Meta setup**
   - What business outcome is being pursued?
   - Which Meta campaign objective and optimization signal best match it in the live account?
   - Is the chosen signal only a proxy, or is it joined to qualified lead / appointment / sale evidence?

2. **Campaign structure**
   - Is campaign → ad set → ad structure as simple as the decision allows?
   - Does each split have a real control/learning reason?
   - Avoid fragmentation that weakens delivery or destroys interpretability.

3. **Audience**
   - Verify live audience controls and current account capabilities.
   - Check geography, language, exclusions/overlap, first-party/custom/lookalike use where lawful and useful.
   - Do not assume manual narrowing is better than Meta automation; require a hypothesis.

4. **Budget, bid, placement, schedule**
   - Confirm budget type, amount, schedule, placement setting and optimization/spending controls in the live account.
   - Treat bid-strategy names and eligibility as volatile.
   - Record what is deliberately controlled versus left to Meta automation.

5. **Creative-to-objective fit**
   - Check that the ad communicates the approved vehicle/offer truth and a clear next action.
   - Evaluate creative for the paid context, not only as an organic Reel.
   - Creative learning must connect to downstream lead quality, not CTR alone.

6. **Policy / category check**
   - Verify current policy state and any Special Ad Category implication when relevant.
   - Finance/credit messaging requires fresh verification; never infer eligibility or terms.

7. **Measurement before optimization**
   - Confirm the actual measurement chain available now.
   - For WhatsApp campaigns, prefer deterministic source stitching when available:
     `Meta ad_id → WhatsApp contact → qualification → appointment/show/test drive → sale`.
   - A Meta conversation count is not a qualified lead count.

## Live campaign monitoring gate

During a campaign, inspect in this order before editing:

`vehicle/offer truth → account/policy state → delivery/spend/pacing → audience → creative → WhatsApp routing → response/qualification → appointment/sale evidence → event/attribution quality`.

Required distinctions:
- impressions/reach = delivery;
- clicks/CTR/CPC = attention diagnostics;
- message starts/conversations = contact proxy;
- qualified lead = Sales-defined downstream state;
- appointment/show/test drive/sale = business outcomes.

Do not upgrade one level into another without evidence.

## Optimization discipline

- Do not edit simply because first-day metrics moved.
- Respect the experiment's observation window and conversion lag.
- Prefer one material variable per learning test when feasible.
- Use A/B testing only when the design can answer a decision-relevant question.
- Do not scale because CTR/CPC/conversation cost looks good if downstream quality is unknown.
- Do not kill a campaign solely because surface metrics are weak if measurement or sales follow-up is broken.
- Separate attributed outcomes from causal incrementality.

## Required evidence for SCALE / HOLD / ITERATE / STOP

A recommendation must state:
- live objective and optimization signal;
- actual campaign/ad set/ad structure;
- audience, geography, placement, budget and schedule state;
- platform delivery metrics;
- deterministic or best-available source attribution;
- number and definition of qualified leads;
- appointment/show/test-drive/sale evidence available;
- conversion lag / observation-window status;
- what single variable is proposed to change, if any;
- authority required before spend or publishing changes.
