## UAE personal-data boundary

UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 creates a personal-data protection framework. Before customer identifiers are uploaded, matched, enriched, exported or reused for Custom Audiences, CRM/CAPI events or similar processing, establish the organization's lawful/approved basis, minimize fields, preserve provenance and suppression handling, and escalate unresolved legal questions.

Technical platform permission is not sufficient evidence of lawful permission.

## UAE telemarketing boundary

Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 and Ministry of Economy guidance create telephone-marketing constraints including DNCR/workflow requirements, approved company calling controls, recordkeeping and permitted calling hours. Distinguish an inbound WhatsApp conversation from later outbound telephone marketing. An ad click or WhatsApp chat is not blanket permission for unrelated marketing calls.

## Meta execution mechanics

Current Meta products can support Leads, click-to-message/WhatsApp acquisition, automation and catalog-based lead generation including vehicle inventory. Treat these as tools, not default strategy.

Choose the deepest feasible optimization target supported by trustworthy signal. Cheap message starts are not equivalent to qualified leads, appointments, shows or sales.

Automotive retail is not automatically a Meta Special Ad Category merely because the product is a vehicle. Exact finance/credit content can create separate policy/classification questions and must be reverified at launch.

Before vehicle-catalog scale verify authoritative inventory, sold/reserved suppression, vehicle identity, price, destination integrity, rejected items and downstream routing that retains vehicle context.

## WhatsApp lead path

For click-to-WhatsApp or messaging acquisition:
- preserve vehicle/offer context;
- keep ad promise and opening conversation continuous;
- qualify only on decision-relevant facts;
- measure response time, qualified conversation, appointment, show and sale where data permits;
- deduplicate repeated contacts;
- preserve original media source;
- do not optimize solely to conversation count.

WhatsApp proactive messaging/template/marketing rules are volatile. Verify current official policy and account capabilities before automation or re-engagement. Do not treat technical deliverability as consent or policy compliance.

## CRM / Conversions API

Downstream events can improve measurement only if event semantics, lawful use, identity matching, deduplication, latency and event quality are valid. Keep lead, qualified lead, appointment, show and sale distinct. Never manufacture sale/purchase events to train delivery.

## Geography and language

Do not choose all-UAE, one emirate, a radius or a language by habit. Use serviceability, travel friction, inventory uniqueness, delivery/export capability, sales-language capacity and lead-to-sale evidence. Geography/language remain hypotheses until internal evidence supports them.

## Diagnostic order

When performance degrades, inspect where relevant:

`inventory truth → Meta delivery/policy/account state → creative/offer truth → capture → WhatsApp routing/response → CRM source/identity → appointment/show capacity → sale outcome → event feedback quality → auction/demand`

Do not increase budget to compensate for a broken feed, stale sold vehicle, slow response, CRM source loss, bad event stream or exhausted sales capacity.

## Authority

The specialist may recommend but must not silently execute spend changes, customer-data uploads, messaging automation, phone outreach, finance claims or catalog changes beyond delegated authority.

## Revalidation triggers

Revalidate when Meta objectives/optimization/catalog/audience/attribution/policies change; WhatsApp policy/capabilities change; UAE regulation changes; account/CRM/workflow changes; or evidence is too old for the decision.
