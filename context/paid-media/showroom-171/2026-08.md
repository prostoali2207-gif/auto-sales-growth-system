## Established business context

- used-car showroom / automotive retail;
- Ajman, UAE; Ajman Auto Market, Showroom 171;
- Instagram is the primary current social/acquisition surface discussed for this business;
- WhatsApp is the primary direct conversation destination discussed for paid acquisition;
- the business does not yet have mature first-party paid-media history;
- human approval/publishing remains part of the operating system;
- the growth system is not yet evidence-complete for authoritative inventory, end-to-end attribution and downstream outcomes.

## No invented economics

Do not invent or infer durable values for gross profit per vehicle, contribution margin, acceptable CAC/CPL/appointment cost, paid-media budget, cash-flow ceiling, marginal CAC or spend authority.

Cheap message CPL, cheap lead CPA, list price or expected sale price do not establish profitability. Until economics are connected, use bounded learning experiments with an explicit business-approved spend ceiling/stop-loss rather than manufactured ROI.

## Inventory truth

Every direct-sale campaign must attach to a verified current vehicle record. Prior reels, captions, chats or old experiments are not authoritative inventory.

At execution time verify where material:
- vehicle identity/model/year/trim/spec;
- current availability/status;
- approved sellable price;
- mileage;
- GCC/import status;
- material accident/repair/paint/condition disclosures;
- finance/warranty claims;
- approved media/current vehicle state.

Sold, reserved, repriced or materially changed vehicles invalidate prior execution assumptions.

## Lead-quality hierarchy

Prefer trustworthy downstream evidence in this order:

`sale / gross contribution → qualified lead → appointment/test drive → show → qualified WhatsApp conversation → raw message/conversation start`

Never scale solely because Meta/Instagram reports cheaper conversations when downstream quality is unknown or degrading.

## Measurement maturity

Default to **measurement incomplete**, not “zero sales”. Until end-to-end linkage is proven:
- preserve vehicle and original source in the lead handoff;
- do not overwrite acquisition source;
- manually reconcile paid leads to downstream outcomes where needed;
- do not claim reliable attribution merely because platform lead counts exist;
- treat missing attribution as missing evidence, not campaign failure.

## Capacity

Current stable values for salesperson count, response SLA, simultaneous WhatsApp load, appointment/test-drive capacity, after-hours ownership and language coverage are not established.

Before any SCALE expected to increase lead volume, explicitly verify that response and appointment capacity can absorb the incremental load without degrading downstream quality.

## Geography and language

Ajman location does not prove Ajman-only targeting. It also does not prove all-UAE, English, Arabic, Russian or CIS targeting.

Use serviceability, buyer travel behavior, delivery/export capability, language handling and lead-to-sale evidence. Small bounded geography/language tests are allowed; large-scale assumptions are not.

## Commercial truth and approval

Price, payment/finance, warranty, accident/paint status, GCC/import status, mileage, availability, shipping/export and condition are commercial facts requiring current evidence and appropriate business approval.

A number from a prior chat, reel, salesperson memory, user suggestion or another agent is not automatically approved truth.

## Experiment-budget discipline

Every paid-media experiment must define:
- decision question;
- maximum approved spend or stop-loss from business authority;
- primary downstream success metric;
- minimum tracking needed for interpretation;
- stop / iterate / scale rule;
- expected learning value.

The specialist may recommend a budget framework but must not invent an approved numeric spend.

## Mandatory SCALE gate

A dealership-context recommendation to `SCALE` is invalid unless it explicitly confirms all three:
1. **marginal business value** — expected marginal acquisition economics remain favorable using verified business economics, not platform average CPA alone;
2. **delegated authority** — the proposed spend increase fits an explicit approved authority limit;
3. **operational capacity** — sales response and appointment handling can absorb expected incremental volume.

If one is unknown, use HOLD / EXPERIMENT / ITERATE / ESCALATE as appropriate instead of SCALE.

## Human/business authority

Human/business authority remains required for at least:
- approved selling price and offer terms;
- conflicting vehicle truth;
- material spend ceiling / stop-loss;
- finance/warranty/condition claims;
- customer-data use where organizational approval is required;
- delivery/export or other operational commitments.

## Explicit unknowns blocking confident scale

- dealership gross/contribution economics;
- approved paid-media budget and delegated spend authority;
- trustworthy paid lead → appointment → sale attribution;
- sales-team response/capacity baseline;
- stable audience/geography/language winner;
- authoritative live inventory integration.

Unknowns do not block every test. They block decisions that require those facts.
