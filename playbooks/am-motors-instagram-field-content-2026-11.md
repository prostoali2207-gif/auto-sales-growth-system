# AM Motors — Instagram field-content operating playbook
Version: 2026-11-21. Status: project-specific operating lessons, NOT a qualified professional core.

## Objective
Build qualified interest and trust for UAE-to-Russia/CIS car sourcing/export; reach and followers are intermediate metrics, not proof of sales. First posts may be discovery/editorial rather than direct sales.

## Evidence discipline
- Separate verified unit facts, external research, working hypotheses, and results of AM Motors experiments.
- Source buyer pains by destination country: RU Drive2/Drom/Auto.ru comments and listings, KZ Kolesa.kz, local YouTube/Telegram/forums and relevant official customs sources; Reddit/Quora primarily for transferable marketing mechanics, not as substitutes for CIS buyer evidence.
- For each reference record original URL, publication date, views and interactions if visible, first 3s, editing mechanism, audience relevance, and limits. No viral/sales claim from views alone.
- Reuse in-repo quantitative UAE automotive Instagram study (200 posts, 5 accounts) as exploratory evidence, not a causal guarantee: research/uae-market/2026-08-11-quantitative-instagram-report.md.
- Market/export benefit requires acquisition, inspection, documents, logistics, insurance, customs, recycling fees, local registration, risk reserve, margin, competitor delivered prices. Different countries separately. Never extrapolate a 2–3L fee to 4.0L. Check official tables and vehicle documents before publishing figures.

## Anti-AI-slop editorial gate
- Reject interchangeable intros, generic 'we will show real cars/prices/condition', theatrical promises, fake intrigue, list-of-three filler, artificial 'not X but Y', generic sales CTAs.
- Every spoken line should be tied to an actual shot, verified fact, observable action or substantive buyer question.
- Prefer colloquial accurate language: 'ходим по рынку, показываем машины с ценами и косяками' when true.
- Introduce company naturally; spoken brand name optional if visible in account. 'Эмираты' more accessible to CIS viewers than Ajman alone; Ajman may remain as concrete filming location.
- Never claim car has no accidents or good condition without proper inspection.
- Avoid promised price/availability, guaranteed import savings, or future episode unless ready to deliver.

## Prado pilot: editorial decision
2022 Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, 4.0 GCC, odometer shown 73,495 km, Ajman market. Video ~23 seconds.
Approved core script:
'Toyota Prado 2022 года. Пробег — 73 тысячи километров. Четырёхлитровый мотор, версия GCC.
Нашли его на авторынке Аджмана.
Вопрос в другом: есть ли смысл везти такой Prado в Россию или СНГ?
Мы работаем здесь, в Эмиратах. Ходим по рынку и показываем машины с ценами и косяками.
Какую машину посмотреть следующей?'
Voice: user's natural voice, lively but not announcer-like; avoid excessive compression, echo, wind, artificial hype. Original car ambience when feasible, no music/whoosh. Tight cuts and audio alignment, check final line not clipped. Subtitles: bold clean white grotesk, high contrast, short semantic phrases, no karaoke/bouncing, don't obscure odometer/VIN. VIN may be visible; don't automatically blur it. Hide personal documents.

## Cover art direction
Use REAL frames of actual car, not generated substitute. Documentary/editorial, restrained typography, legible small grid preview. Hook 'ВЕЗТИ ИЛИ НЕТ?' with 'PRADO 2022 / ИЗ ОАЭ' as needed. Avoid default gold/yellow bars, red paint strokes, arrows, circles, fake logos, glow, excessive specs. Cutout overlap can work if edges clean and text readable. Test 1080x1920 and 3:4 center crop; slight background blur only if still feels like a real market, not fake portrait bokeh. Preserve true vehicle identity.

## Publishing
Caption: real model/year/engine/GCC/mileage + Ajman context + what content viewers will get + one easy open question (model; year optional). 3–5 relevant hashtags as indexing, not reach guarantee. Geotag Ajman; enable highest upload quality; AI label only when applicable to substantially synthetic/altered realistic content; normal edits/captions alone don't imply synthetic scene. Trial Reels is optional, Android supported where account eligible; do not assert missing toggle cause. Check business account/page settings. Cover and captions must survive UI safe zones. No aggressive keyword-comment bait for this discovery post.

Точную сумму и выгоду можно определить только после полного расчёта. Какую машину поискать в Аджмане следующей? Пишите модель.' Recheck current law before reuse.
Reply to relevant comments early. At 24h/48h/7d record non-follower reach, retention/watch time, shares, saves, profile visits, qualified inquiries, calculations, orders, margins. Most-mentioned model is a candidate, NOT automatic next episode; check availability and export economics. Trial Reels can test hooks with disciplined comparable conditions; no causal conclusions from tiny samples.

## Workflow for each next episode
1. Destination/audience and editorial or commercial objective.
2. Verified car fact packet and buyer problem from country-specific listening.
3. Source-backed original references with metrics where available.
4. Script + per-shot mapping + anti-slop critique + claims gate.
5. Shoot/voice/edit/sound/subtitles/cover, mobile grid checks.
6. Publish with truthful caption and one easy discussion prompt.
7. Measure at 24/48h/7d; document result and decide iterate/stop/scale.

## Boundaries
Social Content Creative skill in professional-ai-agents is a candidate/support artifact; do not claim a qualified agent was run unless actually invoked. Market intelligence, strategy, creative, audio editing, and analytics are separate roles. Preserve this file as a field playbook; amend based on measured outcomes rather than unsupported taste.

## Adaptive multi-format system (2026-11-23)
- Twelve Reels remain a learning portfolio, not a fixed calendar or a Reels-only mandate. Test carousels, Stories, and standalone photos when they offer a distinct buyer benefit; do not repeat a Reel as a photo carousel without new information.
- Select next topic by destination-country demand, real Ajman inventory, export landed-cost economics, buyer questions, verifiable evidence and production feasibility; comment count alone is insufficient.
- Assign each publication a purpose: discovery, buyer education, trust/proof, or qualified inquiry. Choose format to fit purpose. No fixed posting frequency or 60/30/10 mix is established by AM Motors evidence.
- Each content test needs a declared audience, hypothesis, main metric, observation window, and decision rule. Change one meaningful variable where feasible; do not attribute changes to a hook or duration after changing several variables together.
- Maintain the publication journal at `analytics/am-motors-instagram-publication-journal.md`. Log 24h/48h/7d snapshots, reach, non-followers, countries, retention, saves/shares, profile visits, qualified inquiries, quotes, orders and margin where available. Missing commercial data is UNKNOWN, not zero.
- For short videos, distinguish average watch time / video duration from Instagram skip rate: numerically similar percentages are not the same metric. Avoid subjective ratings without an explicit rubric.
- Prado #1 evidence (21–23 Nov 2026) shows discovery among Russian and Kazakh viewers, not proven buyer acquisition. Treat early-frame recognition, payoff alignment, practical share value and profile conversion as hypotheses for further tests, not established causes.
