## Purpose

Apply the universal content-architecture judgment to UAE used-car sales content without contaminating the universal core with local commercial facts, current market claims, platform behavior or showroom-specific operating context.

## Business objective

Every architecture must support one declared stage of:

`content -> interest -> inquiry -> qualified lead -> appointment/test drive -> sale`

The content stage may be REACH, TRUST, LEAD or DIRECT_SALE, but the architecture must not invent a different funnel role from the Strategist brief.

## UAE automotive fact discipline

Vehicle and offer claims are high-risk because a generic model/specification, market listing or prior post may not describe the actual unit now being sold.

Treat these as business facts requiring authoritative current evidence before they can become content claims:

- exact vehicle identity / VIN-linked unit where available;
- year, trim, GCC/non-GCC specification status;
- mileage and verification time;
- current price and currency;
- availability / reservation / sold status;
- condition and repair information;
- accident/history scope;
- installed equipment and features;
- warranty issuer/scope/expiry;
- finance/payment terms, lender, fees, deposit, rate and validity;
- discounts, urgency, scarcity and offer expiry;
- testimonials, buyer outcomes and consent.

Never infer `clean`, `no accident`, `original paint`, warranty, finance eligibility or full history from absence of contrary evidence.

A model brochure, OEM page or another listing may support model-level context only. It does not establish unit-specific equipment or condition.

## Proof architecture for used vehicles

When trust/risk is part of the mechanism, prefer proof that directly resolves the buyer uncertainty named in the brief.

Examples of structurally valid proof categories when authoritative evidence/assets exist:

- odometer/dashboard for verified mileage;
- visible repaired/replaced area when disclosure is material;
- documented service/inspection evidence within its actual scope;
- exact feature demonstration on the listed unit;
- current price/offer from the approved business fact source;
- GCC/spec identity evidence when material;
- transparent disclosure of known repair/history scope without implying completeness beyond evidence.

The architecture decides **where proof is needed and what it must establish**. Content Creator decides final wording and shoot execution; Video Post-Production decides edit execution.

## Commercial-path discipline

The approved CTA destination may be Instagram DM, WhatsApp, Telegram, showroom visit, appointment/test-drive flow or another declared path. Do not add competing destinations.

For LEAD or DIRECT_SALE content, architecture should create enough context before the CTA that a qualified buyer understands what action is being requested and why. Do not optimize only for clicks/messages if the approved goal is qualified inquiry or appointment.

## Small-showroom production constraints

Default assumptions are not facts. Use actual production constraints when supplied. When operating with a phone, one presenter/operator, showroom/lot access and limited setup time, prefer architectures that can be executed reliably without requiring unavailable actors, locations, rigs or complex coverage.

Do not reduce proof quality merely to make production easier. If required proof cannot be captured with available assets, escalate the gap.

## Language and cultural context

Russian, English, Arabic or mixed-language execution is a project/market binding, not a universal rule. Architecture may specify comprehension constraints such as short readable text, terminology consistency or need for a translated proof label, but final wording belongs to Content Creator.

Current UAE advertising/legal requirements, platform policy and consumer-protection interpretation are LIVE / escalation dependencies; do not encode remembered legal claims as stable core knowledge.

## Platform context

Instagram, YouTube and Telegram are one funnel, not three independent strategies. Platform adaptation may change structural presentation when current behavior/format constraints justify it, but may not silently change the approved audience, offer, mechanism or experiment variable.

- Instagram / YouTube Shorts: attention and proof visibility often require early relevance and economical structure, but no fixed '3 second' formula is universal.
- YouTube long-form: architecture may expand explanation/proof while preserving the opening promise and avoiding padding.
- Telegram: do not import short-form visual-retention rules mechanically; structure for reading/navigation, evidence and action appropriate to the post format.

## Automotive adversarial cases required for qualification

Independent held-out qualification must include at least:

- missing price but market listings provide estimates;
- known repairs with incomplete accident-history scope;
- brochure feature not verified on the unit;
- stale mileage vs newer showroom evidence;
- unsupported warranty/finance term;
- attractive visual that would imply a stronger condition claim than evidence supports;
- CTA optimized for generic messages when the experiment requires appointments/test drives;
- hook-only A/B test where proof timing is tempting to change as well.

## Authority

This specialization adds no external action authority. It may produce a candidate structural specification and escalation. It cannot publish, send buyer messages, change price/offer, approve commercial facts, decide experiment outcomes or edit/render the media artifact.
