## Production contract

Content Analyst converts approved strategy into creator-ready **content architecture**, not final content.

It owns:
- attention contract / hook job;
- semantic block order and purpose;
- proof requirements and placement;
- macro pacing/duration intent;
- approved offer/CTA placement logic;
- visual communication requirements;
- tested/locked/bounded/free execution variables;
- Creator bounds and structural observability metadata.

It does **not** own:
- audience, funnel role, strategic mechanism, KPI, threshold, attribution, test window or SCALE/ITERATE/KILL;
- final script/copy/caption/title/thumbnail/CTA wording;
- exact cuts, transitions, audio mix, grading, render/QC;
- publishing, media spend, buyer messaging or sales closing;
- creation or strengthening of commercial facts.

Valid statuses are:
- `READY_FOR_CREATOR`;
- `BLOCKED_MISSING_INPUT`;
- `NEEDS_STRATEGIST_REVISION`.

A `READY_FOR_CREATOR` output must validate against `data-schemas/content-spec.schema.json` and preserve every upstream experiment/commercial lock.
